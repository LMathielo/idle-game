## Vision

A mobile idle RPG in the spirit of Gladiatus. One hero, a medieval village hub, gear and attributes that matter, dungeons that fight themselves, and gathering skills that tick while you're away. Short sessions: open, collect, decide, close.

## Pillars

1. **Meaningful decisions, automated execution.** The player chooses what to train, what to equip, where to go. Combat and gathering run on their own.
2. **Always progressing.** Something is always filling up, including offline (capped).
3. **Readable on a phone in one glance.** Pixel-medieval, big tap targets, numbers with deltas.
4. **Server-ready from day one.** Deterministic engine, commands in / state out. Local storage for the POC.

## Platform & constraints

- iOS + Android via Expo (SDK 58), tested through Expo Go. No web.
- Single player for now; multiplayer later via a server that reuses the engine.

## UI rules (2026-10-31)

- **Dialogs:** every confirmation and every alert ("Not possible", "Could not save", errors) is a **GameDialog**: pixel styled, Pixelify title, Inter body, red confirm when the action loses something. Android back or a tap outside = cancel. Never the native alert.
- **Info:** tapping an item or anything with info opens an **InfoPopover**: a squared box next to the tapped element (above or below, kept on screen), with that element's actions as buttons inside. A tap outside closes it. Used for items (Equip / Use / Unequip / Buy / Sell), locked buildings (Build), upgrades (Upgrade), hero stats and attributes, locked dungeons, trees and rocks (ⓘ).
- **Hold-to-repeat:** stamina skips (woodcutting, mining, dungeon search), training speed-ups and the debug buttons (not Reset save) repeat while held: one action on touch, then every 100 ms after 400 ms. A repeat stops on release, when stamina or gold runs out (silently, no dialog), or when the button turns off.
- **Potion Belt (2026-11-06):** the potion button (a flask and the total, bottom right above the strip; a footer row in the Dungeon modal) opens the belt panel, an overlay on that screen (not a native Modal, so dialogs and popovers still open over it): 4 belt slots over the bag's potions; hold a potion and drag it onto a slot, or tap it and then a slot; tap a filled slot for its rules and Remove; cooldown seconds show in green over a slot after a drink.
- **Village map:** drag and pinch-zoom (up to 2.5×), never past the map's edge; it opens centred on the village. Signs scale with the map (their top edge is on their building's bottom edge); the HUD and the activity strip stay fixed on top. Empty roped plots are where future buildings go (the Crafting house is on the east plot).
- **Art and symbols (2026-11-04):** no emoji in any text (titles, strips, summaries, pills, sheets, buttons, notifications, accessibility labels). Where a symbol helps it is a pixel-art image next to the words (gold, stamina, XP, locked, upgrade, crafting, workers, items, enemies, trees, rocks, skills); otherwise the words stand alone. Village signs and dungeon list entries are text only. Plain ✕ ✓ ✗ stay as text. Art is only ever shrunk on device (files ship ×8, the village ×4) and enemies keep one pixel scale (the 48×48 troll shows 1.5× a 32×32 rat).
- **Village (2026-11-04):** a background without buildings plus one sprite per building, drawn back to front. A building that isn't built yet isn't drawn (its road ends on empty ground); its sign stays, greyed with the padlock, and opens the Build box. Decor cottages always show.
- **Building bonuses (2026-11-01):** every building modal (Woodcutting, Mine Shaft, Merchant, Town Hall, Training Grounds, Crafting) shows its **current level's bonuses** as a line under the header, e.g. "Lv3 · −19% craft time", "Lv2 · +20% log yield", "Lv1 · no bonus yet". The Upgrade box keeps its now / next comparison.

## Attributes (2026-11-04, engine 1.19.0)

Plain points are the hero's **total** points in an attribute (spent + extra + core). Core points are the Training level. Plain effects are flat; core effects are a percent multiplier on the final number, `cap × core ÷ (core + 50)`, so core 10 gives a sixth of the cap, 50 half, 100 two thirds. Formulas: ENGINE.md "Attributes & derived stats".

| Attribute | Plain, per point | Core (training level) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strength | +1 physical damage; +1 woodcutting and mining strength | physical damage up to +50% (core 10: +8.3%, 50: +25%) |
| Dexterity | crit damage: 1.5x base, +0.01x per point (2.5x at 100 points) | crit chance up to 40% (core 10: 6.7%, 50: 20%); shorter dungeon searches, cuts and ores (unchanged) |
| Endurance | +5 max HP, +0.2 HP per minute, +1 armor | armor and magic resist up to +50% (one shared bonus) |
| Intelligence | +1 magic damage, +1 magic resist, +5 max mana, +0.2 mana per minute | magic damage up to +50%; shorter crafting (unchanged) |

- A new hero: 12-14 physical damage, 0 magic damage, 13 armor, 0 magic resist, 0% crit chance (1.5x crit damage), 50 HP, 20 mana, 15 HP per minute, 5 mana per minute.
- **Hero strike:** a physical roll in the final damage range, then one crit draw (every strike, even at 0%); a crit multiplies the physical part; armor reduces it. The magic part is the hero's magic damage reduced by the enemy's magic resist; it never crits. A strike deals at least 1. Enemy strikes are unchanged (physical only, no crit).
- **Enemies** get magic resist equal to their armor (Rat 2, Bat 6, Wolf 12, Skeleton 20, Troll 30). No enemy deals magic damage yet (roadmap).
- Plain DEX points do nothing in a fight until some core DEX is trained (no chance to crit); the crit multiplier only matters on a crit.
- Current HP and mana never rise when the max does (a point, a level): the new room fills by regen.

## Difficulty (2026-11-06, engine 1.24.0)
